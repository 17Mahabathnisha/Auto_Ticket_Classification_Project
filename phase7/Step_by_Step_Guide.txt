======================================================================
PHASE 7: STEP-BY-STEP CONFIGURATION GUIDE
======================================================================

STEP 1: ADD SEND EMAIL ACTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Below the conditional branches on the main flow sequence (or after 
   record updates), click "+ Add an Action"[cite: 1].
2. Select "ServiceNow Core" -> "Send Email"[cite: 1].


STEP 2: CONFIGURE DYNAMIC DATA PILLS (DOT-WALKING)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Target Record: Drag the "Trigger -> Incident WorkFlow Record" data pill 
   from the Data Panel on the right[cite: 1].
2. Table: Select "Incident WorkFlow [u_incident_workflow]"[cite: 1].
3. To:
   - In the Data Panel, expand "Trigger -> Incident WorkFlow Record"[cite: 1].
   - Expand the "Caller" reference field (which points to sys_user)[cite: 1].
   - Drag the "Email" field pill into the To input field:
     [Trigger -> Incident WorkFlow Record -> Caller -> Email][cite: 1]
4. Subject: Enter:
   "Your Request for the issue has been submitted."[cite: 1]
5. Body: Enter the standard notification message:

   Hi greetings from xyz college ...!!!

   We are viewed your request and solved by short time period.,
   Thankyou .[cite: 1]

6. Click Done[cite: 1].


STEP 3: ACTIVATE THE FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the upper-right corner of the Flow Designer canvas, click Save[cite: 1].
2. Click Activate[cite: 1].
3. Confirm activation in the modal dialog. The flow status turns to 
   Active (green badge)[cite: 1].
