--------------------------------------------------
⚙️ STEP-BY-STEP CONFIGURATION GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------

### Step 1: Open Form Designer
1. Navigate to the `u_incident_workflow` form list or open a new record.
2. Click the form context menu icon (hamburger icon or right-click form header) -> Configure -> Form Design.

### Step 2: Configure Layout Structure
1. Organize the form into a clean 2-Column section:
   - Left Column: Number, Caller, Category, Subcategory, Short Description, Description.
   - Right Column: State, Assigned group, Assigned to.
2. Drag and drop field types from the left palette onto the canvas.

### Step 3: Configure Choice Options
1. Category Field Choices:
   Click the gear icon (Settings) on the Category choice field and add:
   - Label: Network     | Value: network
   - Label: Hardware    | Value: hardware
   - Label: Access      | Value: access
   - Label: Performance | Value: performance

2. Subcategory Field Choices:
   Click the gear icon on the Subcategory choice field and add:
   - Label: Wi-Fi           | Value: wi-fi
   - Label: Projector       | Value: projector
   - Label: Forgot Password | Value: forgot password
   - Label: Slow Computer   | Value: slow computer

3. Click Save in the top right of Form Designer.
